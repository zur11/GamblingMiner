# Glossary - GamblingMiner

- **Auction Settlement** *(Step 14 ND.5)*: The one-time state-changing event that fires when a *Referral Auction* resolves (`NetworkRoot.TrySettleResolvedAuctions`, called once per live block, diffing the current block's auction ledger against the previous block's so it fires **exactly once** per non-miner). Every donation still in the *Tracked Donation Pool* is revalued at the CLOSING date's (the resolution block's own date) BTC/SC price — uniformly, regardless of each donation's own original date — and each unique donor is paid the SC total, funded from `CasinoScBalanceService.MainBalance` (drawing an on-demand auto-loan first if it can't cover the total). The non-miner then sweeps the pool's total BTC to the casino, network fee deducted from the total (an accepted, documented shortfall — the casino ends up receiving a hair less BTC than the SC it just paid out). See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 22 §22.9. **⚠️ Superseded (Step 14 ND.8b.2, D-ND8.14):** resolution now FOUNDS a company instead — no SC cashback, no BTC sweep; the tracked pool mints the *NST*/*PST* stock distribution and value returns as dividends (§22.12). Kept for history.
- **Auto-Deposit** *(Step 12)*: The automated form of an *SC Deposit* — moves SC **Private Bank Account → Main Balance** when Main can't cover a Bankroll recharge. Default **OFF** (banked SC is a safe reserve); it is a rarely-hit *fallback*, not the primary funding path (early play funds Main→Bankroll as today). Turning it ON at a valid chunk (`0 < amount ≤ bank`) is the opt-in "extra-lazy" streaming. `PlayerBankAccountService.AutoDepositEnabled` / `AutoDepositAmount`.
- **Auto-Withdraw** *(Step 12)*: The automated form of an *SC Withdrawal* — sweeps surplus **Main Balance → Private Bank Account** to lock winnings away safe from the casino. Default **OFF**. Threshold/surplus model: keeps a Main floor (`max(AutoWithdrawThreshold, live recharge dose)` — anti-ping-pong) and moves one `AutoWithdrawAmount` installment per trigger event. This is the shipped "lock in winnings automatically" mechanism (to the bank).
- **Autobet**: Automated betting mode that repeats bets using the current strategy.
- **Bankroll**: Subaccount of Main Balance used for active casino bets.
- **Bankroll Auto-Recharge**: Internal, automated transfer from the player's Main Balance into Bankroll. Fires when Bankroll reaches ≤ 0 SC. Controlled by `BankrollProgramService`; amount is configurable via `BankrollProgrammer`. Does **not** count as an SC Deposit — it is a movement of funds the player already owns. Recorded in `CasinoClientLedgerService` as `kind = "auto_recharge"` (never resets the since-last-deposit baseline). Symmetric to *Main Balance Auto-Recharge* (planned).
- **Bankroll Manual Recharge**: User-initiated transfer from Main Balance into Bankroll, performed via the `BankrollProgrammer` scene. Functionally identical to auto-recharge except it is triggered by the player, not by an exhaustion event. Does **not** count as an SC Deposit — it is an internal movement between the player's own sub-accounts. Symmetric to *Main Balance Manual Recharge* (planned).
- **Block reward**: BTC paid to the miner that finds a valid block.
- **Block template**: Candidate block assembled by a mining node before nonce attempts.
- **Board Vote** *(Step 14 ND.8b.3, D-ND8.18)*: A founded company's shareholder vote — founding-day (at auction close), quarterly (every 3 in-game months), or the >30%-inflow special reserve vote. Runs one in-game day; NST-weighted resolution (D-ND8.19b: weighted average for the reserve %, ~60% supermajority for a ±1 market-category shift). PST carries zero votes. **How the player participates is per-company and opt-in** *(Step 16 P16.5/P16.8, ProjectDesignManual Ch. 41)*: by default a **standing policy** is cast automatically and the game never stops; `PlayerPauseOnVotes` makes that company's votes freeze the whole game until a ballot is registered; `PlayerAutoAbstain` casts nothing at all. Bots also abstain at random (15% per bot per vote, independent — P16.4), and the player can abstain at an individual paused vote via the Abstain toggle. **Abstaining writes no ballot**, so the abstainer's weight leaves the resolver's denominator and every other holder's share rises — which is *not* the same as voting the status quo, a ballot that actively pulls the average toward no change. If nobody votes at all, the reserve holds and the payout rate falls back to the company's category default (`;no_quorum`, P16.7).
- **Bot**: Simulated participant. Some bots are mining nodes; others may only own wallets and transact.
- **Candidate block**: The block a miner is currently trying to solve.
- **Cashback**: Planned return % on losses based on player level.
- **Casino SC Account** *(Step 12)*: The player's SC held **inside** the casino — i.e. **Main Balance + Bankroll** together. Contrasted with the *Private Bank Account*, which holds SC **outside** the casino. Deposits move SC from the bank into this account (Bank→Main); withdrawals move it out (Main→Bank).
- **CasinoFinances**: Planned development scene for tracking casino SC income, payouts, credit line, and debt.
- **Cast Miner** *(Step 14 ND.2)*: A real, named, registry-backed miner bot (`BotWalletRegistry.CastMiners`, e.g. `artforz`, `laszlo`, `foundry_usa`) spawned by the `NetworkPopulationScheduler` as the historical hashrate/address curve grows — the **visible** half of the two-layer historical network model (contrast *Ghost Miner*). Mines founder-style (drained nonce attempts, concurrent with the player's own bets, never advances the clock); wields exactly *Era-Standard Power* per powered member. No SC finances, no betting sessions — does not qualify as a *Qualifying Bidder* in the referral auction. See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 36.
- **Coinbase transaction**: Special transaction that creates the block reward and pays fees to the miner.
- **Company (Founded)** *(Step 14 ND.8b)*: What a *Non-Miner* becomes when its *Referral Auction* resolves (D-ND8.10 — entity = "company", a bid = "backing/a stake"): a named historical business from `Data/Companies/company_roster.csv` that keeps its on-chain BTC as its **treasury**, mints *NST*/*PST* equity to its backers, and runs the dividends/votes economy (*Board Vote*, *Quarterly Dividend*, *Daily Dividend*). See ProjectDesignManual §22.12.
- **Currency Band (CB1–CB5)** *(Step 14 ND.8b, D-ND8.1/3/7)*: A company's fixed reserve-currency identity — CB1 SC-max (100/0) · CB2 SC-lean (75/25) · CB3 balanced (50/50) · CB4 BTC-lean (25/75) · CB5 BTC-max (0/100). A company NEVER changes band; votes move its reserve target only within ±25% around the band default. Deliberately not called "tier" (auction slots) or "category" (market legality).
- **Daily Dividend** *(Step 14 ND.8b.3, D-ND8.17)*: The *PST* holder's payment preference — each elapsed in-game day of a quarter accrues `(profit% × the finalized quarter dividend) ÷ days-in-quarter` into their claimable balance (BTC and SC tracked separately). Continuous liquidity, versus the NST holder's *Quarterly Dividend* lump at quarter end.
- **Difficulty**: Measure of how hard it is to mine a block.
- **Era-Standard Power** *(Step 14 ND.1)*: `BtcNetworkDataService.GetEraStandardPower(date)` — "what one of today's historic miners is worth," a pure function of the calendar date derived from the real hashrate curve, independent of the player's/founders'/any bot's own live power. Also equals the network average at every date by construction (`TotalNetworkUnits(date) / TargetVisibleMiners(date)`). A player at era-standard hardware always represents roughly one *Cast Miner*'s share of the total. See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 36 §36.2.
- **Fee Band** *(Step 14 ND.7)*: The per-day trio of network-fee values the *Historical Fee Replay* exposes — **median** (the day's real median fee, every participant's base; the player's send clamp floor and default-fill), **mean** (`fee_total ÷ tx_count`, paid only by *Cast Miner* sell-flow — they ARE the network's average activity), and **max** (`max(median, mean) × 10`, a documented approximation — no real daily-max metric exists in any confirmed source). Each component carries forward its last positive value across zero/blank days (D-ND7.4); the effective median is an honest **0** from *Market Birth* through 2011-04-13 (no positive median exists in the data before 2011-04-14 = 0.01 BTC). `NetworkFeePolicy.MedianFeeFor/MeanFeeFor/MaxFeeFor` + timestamp twins.
- **Founders**: Satoshi, Hal, and Mike Hearn — special historical nodes that mine (or hold) without betting SC. Satoshi & Hal are **regulated concurrent miners** in the player era (they mine in lockstep with the player's bets, never advancing the clock on their own); Hearn never mines.
- **Game over**: State reached when **all three** SC accounts are empty — *Private Bank Account* + Main Balance + Bankroll = 0 (Step 12 / D-SF2.1). While the bank holds anything it is **not** game over (the player can deposit it back). Written to allow a future BTC→SC coin-swap rescue.
- **Genesis Grant** *(Step 14 ND.8c)*: The canonical starting SC of one of the world's five casino players — `player` and `bot_1..4`, each granted `40,000 SC` (the same `39,900` Main + `100` Bankroll split). Grants are **equity** in the *SC Monetary Ledger*: granted once at world start, never repayable, never debt — the only SC in existence that is not backed by a bank loan. Re-registered on every pre-genesis reset (the client-ledger `"initial"` precedent). Contrast a casino **loan draw**, which mints SC as debt.
- **Ghost Miner** *(Step 14 ND.2)*: The anonymous, session-transient pseudonym (`unknown_miner`, `garage_gpu`, … 12 names) credited with a block mined by the **invisible mass** — the aggregate power term standing in for the rest of the historical network not individually modeled (`max(0, TotalNetworkUnits − player − bots − founders − cast)`). A fresh one-off wallet each session; keys die with the process, so ghost-mined BTC is frozen forever the moment the app closes. Attribution rotates **randomly** among the 12 names (ND.4a) so blocks-mined doesn't read as a synthetic tie. See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 36 §36.3.
- **Hal Finney**: Founder node, the second early miner; keeps one participant's worth of mining power that fades to zero by 9 Aug 2009 (his ALS turning point), then dormant.
- **Halving**: Reward reduction event. Basic Mode uses a scaled interval of 2,100 blocks (≈ 4 in-game years at 100X scale; initial reward 50 BTC; total supply 210,000 BTC).
- **Historical Fee Replay** *(Step 14 ND.7, 2026-07-13)*: The implemented network-fee model (D-14.6's Option A) — real daily historical fees replayed from the network dataset, **retiring Step 10's flat 0.1-BTC / 2009-04-26 era entirely**. The fee era begins at *Market Birth* (2010-07-18), data-driven; from it the day's *Fee Band* governs every transaction fee on the network (fees are fractal-exempt — replayed at face value, never /100). Schedule built and pushed at load by `BtcNetworkDataService.ComputeAndPushFeeSchedule()` into the pure-static `NetworkFeePolicy`; no schedule ⇒ fee-free fallback. Fee semantics are world-defining ⇒ shipped as `WorldFormatVersion` 3 (clean reset). Option B (a reactive fee market from simulated mempool congestion) remains the future validation experiment. See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 36 §36.7.
- **Leading Bid** *(Step 14 ND.4b, refined ND.4d)*: The current highest-standing donation in an in-progress *Referral Auction* — under the ascending-auction rework (2026-07-10), NOT a cumulative sum of everything a bidder has ever sent (that was the superseded EB.2 model). The floor a later donation must clear to take the lead is **asymmetric**: `bot_1..4` need a full band raise over the leader (**5–10%** since ND.10e), but the **player** only needs `leadingBid + 1 satoshi` (a deliberate one-sided exception — the player's cheap raise is easy for the next bot bid to overtake). Either way, taking the lead resets the auction's 20-day countdown. Shown in BlockExplorer's Enroll Mode with its LIVE, current SC value alongside BTC (corrected 2026-07-11 — priced as of now, never a frozen historical-day value). See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 22 §22.7–22.8.
- **Main Balance**: Player reserve outside active betting. This is the preferred user-facing term.
- **Main Balance Auto-Recharge** *(planned — the "lock in winnings" intent is now largely served by Step 12's **Auto-Withdraw**)*: The originally-planned auto transfer from Bankroll back into Main Balance when Main drops below a threshold. Step 12's *Auto-Withdraw* (Main→**bank**) is the shipped realization of "lock in winnings automatically" (arguably better — it parks them outside the casino). A pure Bankroll→Main auto version remains optional/unbuilt. Neither counts as an SC Deposit.
- **Main Balance Manual Recharge**: The **internal** Bankroll → Main Balance transfer, exposed as the "Bankroll → Main Balance" control in `BankrollProgrammer` (implemented). Recorded in `CasinoClientLedgerService` as `kind = "bankroll_withdrawal"` (Step 12 taxonomy fix — an internal sub-account movement, **not** an *SC Withdrawal*, excluded from the withdrawn total). Does not count as an SC Deposit.
- **Market Birth / Trading Unlock** *(Step 13)*: The in-game instant BTC/SC trading becomes possible — **2010-07-18**, Mt. Gox's launch, the first date of the historical dataset. **Data-driven** via `BtcMarketDataService.FirstDataDateLocal` / `IsMarketBorn` — never a second hardcoded date. Before it there is no market, no price, and the swap desk (`CasinoCoinSwaps`) shows only a locked teaser. In the canonical timeline the player waits ~484 in-game days from the 21 Mar 2009 start, which is accepted and historically honest.
- **Market Day** *(Step 13)*: One row of the historical BTC/USD daily dataset (`Data/HistoricalPrices/btc_usd_daily_2010_2025.csv`, 2010-07-18 → 2025-12-31): date, representative daily price (true VWAP or proxy, per `source` — mtgox/bitfinex/binance/none), volume, and trade count. Exposed by `BtcMarketDataService` (O(1) lookups + the `MarketDayChanged` day-boundary event). The day's price holds all day (step function, D-13.2); gameplay reads the **/100 game-scaled** volume/trade accessors (D-13.10), while `price_usd` is exempt from the /100 rule and — since SC is USD-pegged 1:1 — **is** the SC price of 1 BTC.
- **Market Halt Day** *(Step 13)*: A *Market Day* with `source = "none"` — one of 13 real historical trading halts (the June 2011 Mt. Gox hack, the Aug 2016 Bitfinex hack), deliberately preserved in the dataset (never interpolated away). The swap desk closes; the last price carries forward, shown greyed with the historical reason (D-13.11). `BtcMarketDataService.IsHaltDay`.
- **Mempool**: Set of pending BTC transactions waiting to be included in a block.
- **Merkle root**: Hash root derived from the ordered transactions in a block.
- **Mike Hearn**: Founder node who never mines (a receive-only holder); enters ~Apr 2009 and does the famous 32.51 BTC round-trip with Satoshi, ending with +82.51 BTC.
- **Net Worth** *(Step 12)*: The player's total SC across all three accounts — `NetWorthSc = Private Bank Account + Main Balance + Bankroll`. **Overall P/L** = `NetWorthSc − 40,000` (the canonical start). Both are computed in the `ScFinances` controller from the three balance sources (the service stays pure — D-SF2.7); a fresh world reads `0 + 39,900 + 100 − 40,000 = 0`.
- **Non-Miner** *(Step 14 EB.2)*: A holder bot (`non_miner_1`…`non_miner_40`) that never mines — the prize pool of the *Referral Auction*. Introduced gradually along the historical active-address curve from *Market Birth*, capped at a pool of 40 (`BtcNetworkDataService.NonMinerPoolSize`). Funded by automated economy (cast/miner sell-flow, non-miner↔non-miner exchanges) and, once *Recruitable*, competed over by *Qualifying Bidder*s.
- **Nonce**: Value changed by miners while attempting to find a valid block hash.
- **NST (Normal Stock-Token)** *(Step 14 ND.8b.2, D-ND8.6/15)*: A founded company's full-rights share class, minted only for bidders holding a **top-3 auction tier** at close — dividend rights (the quarter-end lump) **and** voting weight (`NST ÷ total NST`). 1 share = `1.00000000 ST` (8-decimal, Money-normalized); base mint = participation% × the 10,000-ST pool, plus the halving slot-bonus ladder. Held forever in Basic Mode (D-ND8.21).
- **Offered Balance** *(Step 13)*: The portion of the casino's own BTC or SC that the swap desk (`CasinoCoinSwaps`) will actually sell or buy right now — `OfferedForSwap(asset) = max(0, CasinoBalance(asset) − StrategicReserve(asset))`. The un-offered remainder is the *Strategic Reserve*. BTC's offered figure excludes any pool-payout money still settling (see `CasinoCoinSwapService.CasinoBtcEquity`) — the casino never offers BTC that belongs to pool contributors. `CasinoCoinSwapService.OfferedBtc` / `OfferedSc`.
- **Account/balance model**: The original testing-stage way balances were computed — summing all confirmed transactions per address. **Superseded** by the UTXO model (Step 8).
- **UTXO model**: The **implemented** transaction model (Step 8). Each output is a discrete unspent "coin"; a spend references prior outputs as **inputs** and creates new **outputs**; balance = Σ of an address's unspent outputs; fee = Σinputs − Σoutputs. Spends coin-select owned UTXOs (combining several when needed) and return **change** to a fresh address. See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 30.
- **UTXO / OutPoint**: An *unspent transaction output* is a spendable coin (an output not yet consumed). An **OutPoint** (txid + output index) is the reference a spend's input uses to point at the UTXO it consumes.
- **Change output**: When a spend's selected inputs exceed `amount + fee`, the remainder is returned to the sender as a second output — sent to a **fresh** derived address, never reusing one.
- **Address non-reuse**: The wallet/privacy practice of using a **fresh address per receive** so transactions aren't publicly linked. In GamblingMiner it is **Satoshi-only** for coinbases (his ~220-address spread); the player/casino/Hal/Hearn only rotate **change** on send. **NOT** the same as the "Patoshi pattern" (see below) — that conflation is the corrected D0 misnomer.
- **Patoshi pattern**: A **mining-forensic fingerprint** (Lerner, 2013) — ExtraNonce/decrementing-nonce/timestamp artifacts that attribute ~22,000 early blocks to one miner. It is about *mining*, not addresses; our engine can't reproduce it (random-nonce search, no ExtraNonce field). Reserved as an optional, **unbuilt** Block-Explorer flavor — do **not** use this term for address non-reuse.
- **Passphrase wallet**: Extra address derived from the same seed phrase plus a chosen passphrase word; enables many distinct addresses per seed.
- **Private Bank Account** *(Step 12)*: An **optional SC reserve the player owns, held outside the casino** (`PlayerBankAccountService`). **Starts empty (`0`)** — the canonical `40,000` stays in the *Casino SC Account* — with all automation OFF by default, so a new player can ignore it for the first in-game months/years. The player moves SC to it (*SC Withdrawal*, Main→Bank, to park winnings safe) and back (*SC Deposit*, Bank→Main, to bring the reserve into play), manually or automatically. Unlike the casino (which borrows on credit), the player *owns* this money — no debt. Managed in `ScFinances`. Future bank dynamics (term deposits, interest) are a planned `ScBank` scene.
- **PST (Preferred Stock-Token)** *(Step 14 ND.8b.2, D-ND8.6/15)*: The second share class, minted for every other bidder still holding any of the 10 tracked tiers at close — dividend rights **with payment preference** (the *Daily Dividend* drip) but **zero votes**. Same base-mint formula as *NST*.
- **Qualifying Bidder** *(Step 14 EB.2/D-EB.7)*: A node whose mining REQUIRES real casino play — the only entities allowed to bid in a *Referral Auction*. Today that means the player and the classic casino-miner-bots `bot_1..4` (bet-driven, hardware-credit-locked mining, exactly like the player). *Cast Miner*s and *Ghost Miner*s do NOT qualify (they mine via drained attempts, never bet, never form a casino relationship) — their transfers are economy, funding a *Non-Miner* without ever starting, leading, or winning its auction.
- **Quarterly Dividend** *(Step 14 ND.8b.3, D-ND8.17)*: The *NST* holder's payout — their full profit-participation share of the quarter's FINALIZED dividend (payout-rate% of each reserve side, set by the quarter-open *Board Vote*; BTC and SC tracked separately), credited as a lump at quarter END ("waiting for quarter-end" is the flip side of the PST drip's liquidity preference). Claimed in `CompanyDetails`; bots auto-claim on-chain.
- **Recruitable** *(Step 14 EB.2)*: A *Non-Miner*'s state once introduced but before its auction resolves (`NonMinerAuctionStatus.InAuction`) — eligible to receive donations and, from a *Qualifying Bidder*, competitive bids. A never-bid-on non-miner stays recruitable indefinitely; once `Resolved`, a win is **permanent** and never reopens.
- **Referral Auction** *(Step 14)*: The competition for each *Non-Miner* as a permanent casino referral — whoever holds the *Leading Bid* when the countdown expires wins it forever. Reworked 2026-07-10 (ND.4b/ND.4c) into a real ascending auction: an opening bid, a minimum raise over the current leader, and a rolling 20-day window that resets on every accepted raise. The opening bid is **price-anchored** — worth `$0.10`, capped at `1 BTC` (ND.10e, superseding the original flat 0.1 BTC) — and the bot raise band is **5–10%** of the leader (ND.10e, halved from 10–20% so bots price out at half the old speed). See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 22.
- **RTP**: Return to Player. Dice currently targets roughly 99.02% RTP.
- **Satoshi Nakamoto**: Founder node, the dominant early miner; power-regulated to ~10% of blocks toward a 11,000-BTC target by 26 Apr 2011, then retires (coins frozen in Basic Mode).
- **Saturation Ladder** *(Step 14 ND.6)*: The casino-miner-bots' (`bot_1..4`) probabilistic re-bidding rule in the *Referral Auction* — when a chosen bot already participates in a pool, it re-bids there only with a probability set by its best *Tier* in that pool's *Tracked Donation Pool*: tier 4 → 5%, 5 → 8%, 6 → 13%, 7 → 21%, 8 → 34%, 9 → 55% (consecutive Fibonacci percentages, a literal constant table — `NetworkRoot.ReBidProbabilityPercentByTier`). This is the NORMAL table, for pools with ≥ 7 occupied tracked slots; younger pools use the steeper EARLY-RUSH table (tier 4 → 34%, 5 → 55%, 6 → 89% — ND.6d, 2026-07-14), and a NORMAL pool whose rolling window is inside its **final 7 in-game days** rolls the URGENCY table instead — each tier one Fibonacci level up (tier 4 → 8% … 9 → 89% — ND.6e, 2026-07-15, Option B: challenges cluster into late-window "sniping"; an accepted raise resets the window and calms the pool back down). The "satisfied" band was **narrowed at ND.8d** (2026-07-20) from a flat top-3: **tier 1 is always satisfied** (the leader never re-bids — last-bid preservation), **tier 2 is never satisfied**, and **tier 3 only once the bot holds ≥2 of its own slots there**; tiers 2/3 roll their own shallow, bid-count-aware table instead. Tier 10 is excluded by the self-eviction guard (bidding into a full pool where your own donation is the smallest would evict it) — which is why tier 10 has no table entry. **A lone occupant below the leader also escalates**: a bot holding exactly one slot rolls `max(mode rate, escalation)`, where the escalation grows by the tier's own slope each block it stays stuck (ND.8d round 3; slopes ordered `t3 < t2 < t4 … < t9` and capped at 34% inside the top-3 NST band — ND.10i). **Rolls are parallel, not singular** (ND.10c, superseding "one roll per slot"): every affordable qualifying pool rolls its own probability each donation slot, and if several hit, one wins a **weighted** tie-break (ND.10l — an unbid pool weighs `FreshPoolSeedingWeight = 34`, not its `r = 100` sentinel). Replaced the hard top-5 filter that had structurally stalled all bot bidding (leaving every player bid uncontested). See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 22 §22.10 and §22.14.
- **SC**: Stable Coin, simulated USD-pegged currency.
- **SC Deposit**: SC entering the player's **Main Balance** from **outside the casino**. Since Step 12 the canonical home is the **`ScFinances`** scene (the retired `DepositPopup` used to be the only path); a deposit is a **Private Bank Account → Main Balance** transfer, either **manual** (`TriggerManualDeposit`) or **auto** (`TryAutoDeposit`, the opt-in fallback). Critically distinct from a *Bankroll Auto-Recharge* / *Bankroll Manual Recharge*, which move funds already inside the casino between sub-accounts; a deposit brings SC back into play from the reserve. Recorded in `CasinoClientLedgerService` as `kind = "initial"` (the starting `40,000` stake) or `kind = "deposit"` (later Bank→Main deposits), with `Method` = `"manual"` | `"auto"`; both `initial` and `deposit` (manual **and** auto — D-SF2.2) reset the since-last-deposit metric baseline.
- **SC Monetary Ledger** *(Step 14 ND.8c — monetary-system Option 0)*: The world's mint/burn accounting for SC (`ScMonetaryLedgerService`, autoload #18): every event where SC **enters or leaves existence** — *Genesis Grant*s (equity) and casino bank **loan draws** (debt); **burns** (repayment destroying SC) are reserved for the ND.8e Central Bank. Standing invariant: `total SC in circulation = genesis grants + outstanding debt` — the debt-backed dollar in bookkeeping form. Flows between existing holders (bets, transfers, swaps) are NOT mint/burn and stay with their own ledgers. DEV readout in the `WorldEconomy` scene. Simulates SC **quantity/credit only, never value** — the 1:1 USD peg is canon (monetary tightening = credit scarcity, never inflation). See ProjectDesignManual Ch. 36 §36.9.
- **SC Withdrawal** *(Step 12)*: SC **leaving the casino** — a **Main Balance → Private Bank Account** transfer, either **manual** (`TriggerManualWithdrawal`) or **auto** (`TryAutoWithdraw`). Managed in `ScFinances`. Recorded in `CasinoClientLedgerService` as `kind = "withdrawal"` (with `Method` manual/auto) and counts toward "Total SC withdrawn". **Not** to be confused with the *internal* Bankroll → Main movement, which is `kind = "bankroll_withdrawal"` (an internal sub-account transfer, excluded from the withdrawn total — the Step 12 taxonomy fix).
- **Simulacrum (DEV Alt-Timeline)** *(Step 13)*: A **throwaway development world** whose entire early history is shifted forward **+484 days** (genesis 2010-05-02, player landing 2010-07-18) so swap tooling could be built against live market data without the canonical ~16-month wait for *Market Birth*. Controlled by one compile-time flag (`TimelineConfig.DevAltTimeline` — **`false` on `main`, forever**); a stamp + guard (`user://world_timeline.stamp`, `NetworkRoot.ResetWorldIfIncompatible`) auto-wipes the world on any timeline switch, both directions. **Never canon** — worlds across timelines are mutually incompatible; exited at Step 13 TL.3. Re-mount / new-bootstrap design guide: ProjectDesignManual Ch. 35.
- **Stop on block mined**: Strategy condition that stops betting after a block is mined.
- **Stop on loss/profit**: Strategy condition that stops betting after session loss or profit reaches a configured value.
- **Strategic Reserve** *(Step 13)*: The portion of the casino's BTC or SC that the swap desk deliberately holds back from sale/purchase — a manual %/amount setting (BTC in `CasinoFinances`, SC in `CasinoGamblingFinances`) plus, for SC only, an optional auto-computed floor ("R2, recharge pace" — see ProjectDesignManual Ch. 33) composed as `EffectiveReserve = max(manual, auto)`. The BTC reserve is a war chest held for when SC runs low; the SC reserve protects the float the Bankroll's auto-recharges draw from. `CasinoCoinSwapService.BtcReserve` / `ScReserve` / `EffectiveScReserve`.
- **Swap** *(Step 13)*: A player↔casino trade of SC for BTC ("Buy BTC", Panel A) or BTC for SC ("Sell BTC", Panel B) at the `CasinoCoinSwaps` scene. The casino acts strictly as a dealer, never a market-maker with invented liquidity — it only sells BTC and buys SC out of its own *Offered Balance*. The SC leg settles instantly; the BTC leg is a real on-chain transfer that confirms at the next mined block (shown as a pending row until then). `CasinoCoinSwapService.TryExecuteScToBtc` / `TryExecuteBtcToSc`.
- **Swap Fee** *(Step 13, additive model since 2026-07-08, D-SW.11)*: The casino's margin on every *Swap* — one percentage (default `10%`, dev-clamped `1%–10%` in `CasinoGamblingFinances`) applied to BOTH swap directions, charged as a SEPARATE, additive line item alongside the 0.1 BTC on-chain network fee (`totalFee = 0.1×(1+fee%) + fee%×base`) — the two are summed, never `max()`'d, so the casino's real margin is always at or above the nominal %, never below it. Rank-ready: every quote/execution reads it through `CasinoCoinSwapService.GetSwapFeePercentFor(clientId)`, the one hook a future rank system would override.
- **Tier** *(Step 14 ND.6)*: A tracked donation's 1-based position in a non-miner's *Tracked Donation Pool*, ordered by BTC value — tier 1 = the largest donation … tier 10 = the smallest slot of a full pool. Drives the *Saturation Ladder*. Deliberately **not** called "rank": that word is reserved for the future (yet-undesigned) casino ranking system and must not be used for auction positions.
- **Tracked Donation Pool** *(Step 14 ND.5)*: A non-miner's GLOBAL top-10-by-BTC-value ordering of every qualifying donation it has ever received (win-or-lose bids alike — not just the *Leading Bid*), shared by every donor competing for it (no per-donor reservation). A new donation joins if fewer than 10 are tracked, or evicts the current smallest if strictly larger (a tie never evicts — first-in stays); evicted/never-tracked BTC becomes the non-miner's own property forever, excluded from the eventual *Auction Settlement*. **Three kinds of qualifying bid never enter the pool at all** (no slot, no stock, and none of them refunded): a bid from the party that is already the *Leading Bid* holder (ND.8d.6, last-bid preservation), a bid confirmed after the window closed (ND.8d.7 — that one IS refunded, from the founded company's treasury), and **every bid but the highest that one donor made in a single block** (ND.10k, 2026-07-28 — without it two same-block bids from one party could take tiers 1 *and* 2, buying two slot-bonus entries for the same total BTC).
